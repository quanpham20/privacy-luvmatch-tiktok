TikTok User Data Deletion Instructions

Application: LuvMatch • TikTok Client Key: awfojt5u0tl4q8ho

Overview

According to the TikTok Developer Terms of Service, users who log into LuvMatch using their TikTok credentials have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of all data associated with their account.

You can request the deletion of your TikTok data using any of the following 3 methods:

1️⃣ Method 1: Revoke Access in TikTok App (Recommended)
  1. Open the TikTok app on your mobile device.
  2. Go to your Profile > tap the Menu (3 lines) in the top right > tap Settings and privacy.
  3. Tap on Security & permissions > select Manage app permissions.
  4. Find LuvMatch in the authorized apps list and tap on it.
  5. Tap Remove access to revoke token access.
2️⃣ Method 2: Permanent Account Deletion in LuvMatch App
  1. Open the LuvMatch mobile application.
  2. Go to Profile > tap the Settings icon.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account Permanently.
  4. Confirm your choice. Your account, profile photos, matches, and TikTok Open ID will be permanently removed immediately.
3️⃣ Method 3: Direct Email Request to Developer

Send an email request to our support team:

• Subject: TikTok Data Deletion Request - LuvMatch
• Information: Provide your TikTok username or LuvMatch registered account.
